Our Philosophy

We don't make spirits.
We make patience drinkable.

Empyrean Spirits began with a single cask and a refusal to compromise. Today, our four expressions span continents, from Highland barley to Caribbean cane, but the principle remains unchanged: source obsessively, distill slowly, age honestly.
